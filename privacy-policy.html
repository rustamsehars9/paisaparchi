Privacy Policy

PaisaParchi · Effective 16 November 2026

Summary

PaisaParchi is an offline invoice and bookkeeping app. It does not collect, store on any server, share or sell your personal or business data. The app has no internet permission, no account sign-up, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

Information you enter

Business details, clients, products, invoices, quotations, payments, expenses, your logo, signature and receipt photos are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. We cannot see or access this information.

Permissions

• Notifications — to remind you about invoices that are due (optional). • Run at start-up — to keep those reminders scheduled after the phone restarts. • Fingerprint / face — to unlock the app if you turn on App lock (optional). Biometric data never leaves your phone and is handled entirely by Android. • Camera and photos are used only when you choose to attach a receipt or logo, through Android's own camera and photo picker.

Sharing and exports

Files leave your device only when you choose to share or save them — for example sending an invoice PDF on WhatsApp, exporting a CSV, or saving a backup file. Where those files go is controlled by you and by the app or service you pick.

Backups

Backup files are created only when you ask for one and are stored wherever you choose. If Android's own device backup is turned on in your phone settings, Android may include this app's data in your Google account backup; this is managed by Google, not by PaisaParchi.

Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes all app data permanently, so keep a backup file if you need your records.

Children

PaisaParchi is a business tool intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in the app and on our policy page with a new effective date.
