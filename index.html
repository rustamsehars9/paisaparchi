FAQ

Questions, answered

Is PaisaParchi free?

Yes, you can download it free from Google Play and start making invoices right away.

Does it need internet?

No. Everything — invoices, PDFs, reports and backups — works fully offline. The app does not even have internet permission.

How do I send an invoice on WhatsApp?

Open the invoice and tap the green WhatsApp button. The PDF is attached automatically — just pick the chat.

Can I add GST, discount and delivery charges?

Yes. Every invoice supports discount (percentage or amount), tax such as GST and delivery charges. Set your default tax once in settings.

What happens if I change my phone?

Create a backup file from Backup & restore, keep it on Google Drive or WhatsApp, then restore it on your new phone after installing PaisaParchi.

Can I turn a quotation into an invoice?

Yes, with one tap. All items are copied and the two documents stay linked.
